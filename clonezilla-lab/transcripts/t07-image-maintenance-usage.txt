To run ocs-chkimg:
ocs-chkimg [OPTION] IMAGE DEVICE
IMAGE is the image dir name, not absolute path
DEVICE is the device name (not whole disk, only partition), e.g., sda1, sda2, or /dev/sda1, /dev/sda2...
